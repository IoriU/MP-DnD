      How to play
      • The map: click a glowing room to vote. Majority wins; ties are rolled. Beat 3 acts, each ending in a boss 👑.
      • Rooms: ⚔️ fight · 💀 elite (tough, lots of XP, drops gear) · ❓ event · 🔥 rest · 💰 treasure (pick a relic) · 🛒 shop.
      • Gear, bag and relics 🎒: every hero wears a weapon, armor and a trinket, and carries up to 8 more items (gear and consumables). Press I between fights to equip, give or drop things. Consumables show up on your hotbar and are used up. Relics (the icons at the top of this panel) help the whole party for the rest of the run. Gold is shared: spend it at 🛒 shops on gear, consumables, a relic, potions, lessons (upgrade a spell) or forgetting a skill, and sell spare gear for half.
      • In a fight: on your turn you get movement (blue tiles) and ⚡ 3 AP (action points). Attacks and most spells cost 2 AP, quick skills 1, the biggest spells 3, and a few are free. AP you don't spend carries over to your next turn (a turn starts with at most 5), so you can save up for two attacks. Click a tile to walk (or WASD, Q E Z C for diagonals); walking is free. 👟 Dash (everyone, 1 AP) gives 3 more tiles.
      • 🤝 Shared turns: heroes next to each other in the turn order play at the same time. Each of you moves and acts with your own movement and AP, in any order, and ends your own turn; the monsters wait until all of you have.
      • 🧱 Block: soaks damage before your HP (and before temporary HP), until the start of the turn of whoever gave it. Brace (everyone, 1 AP, once per turn) gives you 1d6; Fighters and Paladins have bigger Block skills, and some gear puts up Block every turn. Skeletons, Hobgoblin Captains and Bone Knights carry shields too: hover a monster to see its Block.
      • Attack: click a monster to use your basic attack (free, no limit). Skills: keys 1–9 and 0 (or click), then click a target. Area skills show what they hit when you hover a tile.
      • Facing and back attacks: every creature faces the way it last moved or attacked (the little arrow on its ring). Attack from the 3 tiles behind it and the roll has advantage. Monsters can do the same to you, so watch your back.
      • Flanking: attack in melee while a friend stands on the tile directly across from the target and you have advantage. Monsters flank too. On your turn, gold dots on the blue tiles show where you would get advantage; hover a dot to see why. Hover a monster to preview advantage or disadvantage before you attack.
      • 🛡️ Cover: crates and rubble (low cover) block movement but not arrows; standing next to one, or next to a wall, on the side a shooter is coming from makes you harder to hit: +2 AC behind low cover, +5 behind a wall, and the same on DEX saves against blasts from that side. Barrels and braziers count as low cover too. Melee and flanking ignore it. Hover a tile you can walk to and shields mark its sides that have cover (a whole shield for a wall, a half one for low cover); hover a monster to see the cover it has against you.
      • 🤫 Sneak or ⚔️ Charge: before most fights the party votes. Sneak is a group Stealth check (each hero rolls d20 + DEX, Rogues add their proficiency; at least half must pass): win and the monsters are Surprised and lose their first turn, fail and you are. Charge is a normal start. Boss fights and events skip the vote. Some fights are ⚠️ ambushes: you start Surprised. The ⏰ Alert passive can't be surprised.
      • 🗺️ Big rooms, different starts: fight rooms are big, with walls, crates and rubble to fight around, and fights don't always start the same way (the log tells you when it isn't the line): the line (you on the left, them on the right), scattered groups, a pincer (they close in from both ends), surrounded (every ambush starts like this) or their camp (they wait in the middle and you come in from three sides). Bosses always start as a line.
      • Party: every party has at least 3 heroes: bots take the empty seats when the run starts (alone, you get two bot companions). Bots have their own voices and shout in the log as they fight (low on health, a friend down, a kill steal, a critical hit). Bigger parties face more monsters, with more HP and harder hits.
      • Difficulty: the host picks 📖 Story, ⚔️ Normal or 💀 Hard in the tavern, and the whole run keeps it (it shows next to the act name). On Story the monsters are weaker and fight on instinct: each one goes for whoever is closest. On Normal and Hard they fight as a squad, and Hard hits harder and brings more of them. On Hard every regular monster also has one extra move (hover a monster: 💀 On Hard): goblins hamstring, skeletons shield-bash, zombies grab, necromancers raise the dead...
      • 🧠 Who plays the monsters: the host can pick the 📜 scripted squad AI or a 🧠 PPO-trained policy (a neural network trained against the hero bots) in the tavern, when the server has one. It keeps the same difficulty and moves, but chooses its own way, and the run keeps the pick (a 🧠 PPO tag shows next to the difficulty).
      • 🎯 Monster squads: on Normal and Hard the monsters call a target and all go for it. A red 🎯 reticle marks each hunted hero (a big party gets several packs, each hunting its own hero) and the turn order names them, so heal them, block for them or draw the monsters off. Every monster has a role (hover one to read it): 🛡️ brutes hold the front, 🗡️ skirmishers circle for flanks and back attacks, 🏹 archers keep their distance, ✨ supports (Goblin Shaman, Necromancer, Dragon Priest) heal and buff from the back, and 💨 cowards run when they are hurt. Some shove you into fire. They also shout while they do it, in italics in the log. Kill the supports first.
      • 👤 Hide (Rogue cantrip, 1 AP, not next to an enemy): monsters go for someone else if they can, and your next attack has advantage and gives you away.
      • ✋ Shove (everyone, 1 AP): a strength contest against a monster next to you. Win and it is pushed 1 tile, or 2 if you win by 5 or more.
      • 🕸️ Monster tricks: monsters set each other up. A Kobold throws a 🪢 net (you can't move, and archers get advantage on you), a Giant Spider spits a 🕸️ web (you're stuck and easy to hit), a Goblin throws sand in your eyes, a Dragon Cultist 🎯 brands you for the whole pack, Wolves and Ogres knock you 🤕 prone (melee advantage until you get up), and a Wight's touch 🩸 lowers your max HP until the fight ends. Any monster next to you can 👉 get in your face so the next attack on you has advantage, and leaders (captains, chiefs and bosses) shout orders: 📯 Charge!, 🛡️ Hold the line! or 🎯 Kill that one!. Hover a monster to see its tricks. ✂️ Cut Free (everyone, 1 AP) gets you or a friend next to you out of a net or a web, and fire burns a web away. Take out the trick-throwers and leaders first.
      • ⏱ Cantrips (top row) have a cooldown in your turns and are ready again at the start of every fight.
      • 🔮 Spells (second row) are strong but cost slots (the pips). Only a Long Rest restores them, so save them for the tough fights.
      • 🔁 Passives are always on. Hover an icon to read it.
      • XP: everyone shares each kill, the killing blow earns extra and so does every assist; healing and buffing allies earn XP too. Land a killing blow on a monster someone else mostly beat down and it's a 🗡️ kill steal.
      • Spoils 🏆: after every won fight each hero picks 1 of 3 cards: two skills (a new one or an upgrade) and an item (gear or a consumable, now and then a boon instead). Skill cards have rarities like items, and every class card belongs to one of your class's 3 build engines: picks lean toward the engines you've started, and an epic capstone only shows up once you own 2 cards of its engine. The victory screen waits until everyone clicks Continue, so pick there; the panel in the corner (L opens and closes it) works any time after, even mid-fight. Your hotbar holds 3 cantrips and 3 spells, and passive slots are limited, so a new one means forgetting an old one. 🧪 Potions can drop after a fight too: a 40% chance, and the odds climb after every fight without one.
      • Level up ⬆️: levels give you HP and power and nothing else. Your build comes from the spoils you pick.
      • Elites 💀, blessings and allies: elites carry traits (hover one to read them: a Shielded ward, a Burning aura, Swift, and more). Beat one and every hero picks an elite spoil (a skill upgrade, a piece of gear or a boon), then the party votes on a ✨ blessing (a perk for everyone until the end of the act) or an 🤝 NPC ally who fights for you for a few fights. Allies wear a green ring: you can't attack them, but your area attacks can hit them.
      • Rest sites 🔥: everyone picks Long Rest (heal 30% of max HP and restore all slots) or Train (upgrade a cantrip or spell by a tier). Undecided heroes take a Long Rest when the timer ends.
      • Dice: attacks roll d20 + bonus vs the target's AC. A natural 20 crits.
      • At 0 HP you fall and make death saves. A friend next to you can 🤝 Help Up (2 AP: back up at 25% of your max HP), and healing spells, potions and scrolls get you up too, but cantrips like Lay on Hands can't. Whoever gets you up, you're 🥴 Groggy and lose your next turn. Three failed death saves and you die: you're back after a won fight, but 🩹 Injured (−20% max HP for each death) until the next rest site. Each time you fall again in the same fight, your death saves start one failure worse, and the 4th fall is death.
      • 👑 Bosses are a race. When a boss falls, the rest flee. Each one punishes a slow fight its own way: the Goblin King's 📯 War Horn calls more goblins every 3rd round, the Bone Knight's 🦴 Rise Again stands its fallen back up, and the Dread Wyrm's 😡 Wrath makes it angrier every round from round 4. Burst it down.
      • Rarity: grey common, green uncommon, blue rare, purple epic, orange legendary. Rare things stay rare: an epic is about 1 in 100 in act 1, more in later acts, and every common item card makes the next rare a little likelier. Elite spoils come in a crimson panel.
      • Between rooms: HP carries over. Drink potions, or heal friends by picking a heal and clicking their card.
      • Keys: Space end turn · Esc cancel aiming · I bag · L spoils and level-up · P auto-play · G pixel art or classic emoji look · Enter chat.
